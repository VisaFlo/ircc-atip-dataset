#### Before You Begin

##### Who is this course intended for?

The blended online and in-class course, Removals, is an essential component of your Inland Enforcement Training Program. This course is designed for Inland Enforcement Officers who will be enforcing removal orders under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act (IRPA). Created for use nationwide, it is expected that instructors will adapt in-class content to regional variations.

##### Purpose of the Course

The two-part course provides you with an in-depth look at the removals function. Removals (in-class) builds on the learning you acquired in the on-line section. ■ The full course covers both removals legislation and on-the- job procedures, and promotes the successful and legal enforcement of removal orders with procedural consistency across regions.

##### Learning Objectives

After completing this blended course, you will be able to identify when a removal order is enforceable and to effectively make arrangements for a removal.

Prerequisite

You are required to have completed the online component of the course before you are eligible to register for the in-class session. ？

4.2 Review
The policy basis for assessing risk prior to removal is found in Canada's domestic and international commitments to the principle of protecting people from persecution and cruel and unusual punishment.

This principle holds that persons should not be removed from Canada to a country where they would be at risk of persecution, torture, risk to life, or cruel and unusual treatment or punishment.
