## What is Module 3 - Decision Maker Module?

The Module 3 - Decision Maker Module is a tool that aims to increase the quantity of decisions that an officer can make on any given day as well as improve the quality of those decisions. Increased efficiency is gained by bringing a range of pertinent information required for making decisions together in one place where it can be reviewed easily and effectively. Module 3 is organized in such a way that efficiencies of scale can be produced by processing like cases together and facilitating grouping in the finalization process
