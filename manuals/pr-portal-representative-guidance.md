Guidance for applications submitted by representatives through the Permanent Residence (PR) Portal

Applicants are required to sign and submit their complete application, including all mandatory forms, fees, information and supporting documentsthrough the PR Portal.

If an application is submitted by a representative through thePR Portal, it shouldbe deemed incomplete consistent with the requirements under section R10(b) of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations (IRPR), as the application must be signed by the applicant. The intake office shouldreject applications submitted through the PR Portal in thefollowing cases:

1. There is a signed and dated IMM 5476 on file appointing a representative, with the exception of an uncompensated representative who is a friend or family member and the e-mail address in the portal account matches the business email address of the representative listed on the IMM 5476 form.
2.The e-mail address in the portal account matches the business email address of an authorized representative (compensated or uncompensated).
3. Applications for multiple different unique clients submitted using a single a portal account.
If the applicationdoes not meet the requirements pursuant to section R10 and is found to be incomplete), the agent rejects the application and returns it to the applicant, along with the fees and records the action in GCMS.We recommend using the following template to convey the rejection of the application to the client.

## (1.0) Overview

### (1-A) Introduction

(1-A.1) What is the Online PR Intake Portal? The Permanent Residence (PR) Online Application Portal is a digital solution that was developed in partnership with a third party (Deloitte), allowing clients to submit their applications digitally in a Protected B security environment. It is a digital alternative to the current paper intake application process and allows PR applicants to create their account, fill out digitized forms, upload other IRCC forms with supporting documentation, and electronically sign their application before submitting it online. The Caseworker Portal enables caseworker agents to log in, retrieve an application, and conduct a completeness check. As there is currently no integration with GCMS, manual data entry will be required to create applications in GCMS and to upload supporting documents when an application has been deemed complete. If an application is deemed incomplete, the intake offices will no longer need to return the whole application package back to applicants. Only the rejection letter will be sent to clients as they will have access to their original submission in the Portal. The PR application portal is developed with multiple lines of businesses in mind, therefore, it is not specific to PGP
